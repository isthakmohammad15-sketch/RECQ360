# RECQ360

### AI-Powered Disaster Preparedness & Emergency Response Command Platform

RECQ360 is an AI-powered emergency management and disaster preparedness platform designed to help municipal authorities monitor, analyze, and respond to disaster situations in real time.

The platform provides a centralized command dashboard for monitoring risk conditions, affected zones, emergency resources, shelters, alerts, and response operations across multiple administrative zones.

---

## 🚨 Problem

During natural disasters such as cyclones, floods, and severe weather events, authorities need to make fast and accurate decisions using information coming from multiple sources.

Traditional emergency management systems can make it difficult to:

- Monitor multiple zones simultaneously
- Identify high-risk areas quickly
- Track emergency resources
- Coordinate response teams
- Monitor shelter capacity
- Prioritize critical incidents
- Communicate alerts efficiently
- Make data-driven decisions during rapidly changing situations

RECQ360 addresses these challenges through a unified AI-assisted command and response platform.

---

## 💡 Solution

RECQ360 brings disaster monitoring, risk analysis, emergency coordination, and decision support into a single platform.

The system is designed around a centralized command center where authorities can:

- Monitor all administrative zones
- View real-time risk information
- Identify critical areas
- Track emergency resources
- Monitor shelters and capacity
- Manage alerts and incidents
- Coordinate response operations
- Receive AI-powered recommendations
- Prioritize emergency actions

---

## 🤖 AI Assistant — RECA

RECQ360 includes an AI-powered emergency response assistant called **RECA**.

> **RECA — Response & Emergency Coordination AI**

RECA acts as an intelligent decision-support layer for emergency management.

It can help authorities understand the current situation, identify priorities, summarize incidents, and provide actionable recommendations.

### Example

Instead of manually checking multiple sections of the dashboard, an officer can ask:

> "Which zones require immediate attention?"

RECA can analyze the available information and respond with prioritized areas and recommended actions.

### RECA capabilities

- Emergency situation summarization
- Risk prioritization
- Zone-level analysis
- Incident analysis
- Resource monitoring
- Shelter status analysis
- Alert prioritization
- Response recommendations
- Natural-language interaction

- 🤖 AI-powered decision support
- 📈 Analytics and reports

---

## 🏙️ Designed for Municipal Disaster Management

RECQ360 can be adapted for municipal authorities managing multiple administrative zones.

For the current prototype, the platform is designed around a **10-zone municipal emergency management model**.

Each zone can be monitored independently while still providing authorities with a unified city-wide overview.
